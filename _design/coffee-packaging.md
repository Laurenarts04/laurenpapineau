## Project Overview

This coffee series project was created to offer the opportunity to explore a new stylistic direction or
continue with an existing style for the creation of label artwork.


## Tools & Techniques

- Adobe InDesign for layout
- Adobe Illustrator and Procreate for illustrations and colour
- Design packaging inpsired by italian playing cards

## Outcomes

This project helped push my artisitc style when creating an illustrative label packaging. The final illustrations were then placed on a mock up of coffee bag packages.
