# Project Overview

This project was to create a custom corporate brochure with a short fold panel. The client,
had requested that the design match the lifestyle of his clients and must have a true alternative,
hardcore feel.


## Tools & Techniques

- Adobe InDesign for layout
- Adobe Photoshop for texture, blending modes & resizing
- Adobe Illustrator for illustrtations 

### Outcomes

This project helped me further understand the importance of production when designing for print.
